# K's Project Hub

Install K's macros and keep them up to date — one click.

## Get it

**Windows**
1. Download **[KsProjectHub.exe](https://github.com/Acor1n/Ks-Project-Hub/releases/latest/download/KsProjectHub.exe)**
   and put it somewhere you'll find it again (like your Desktop).
2. Open it. If Windows says *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info → Run anyway**
   (new programs from small creators get this until enough people have run them).
3. Press **Install** on the macro you want, then **Open**.

**Mac** (Apple Silicon: M1, M2, M3, M4)
1. Download **[KsProjectHub.dmg](https://github.com/Acor1n/Ks-Project-Hub/releases/latest/download/KsProjectHub.dmg)**
   and open it, then drag **K's Project Hub** onto **Applications**.
2. Open it from Applications. The first time, macOS says it can't check the app: open
   **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
3. Press **Install** on the macro you want, then **Open**. A macro that isn't out for Mac yet
   says *Coming to Mac soon*.

When K puts out an update, the Hub shows **Update** — press it (close the macro first).
The Hub updates itself the same way.

## Safe by design

- Every update is signed by K. The Hub refuses anything that isn't, and checks every download
  byte for byte before it replaces anything.
- Your settings (slots, auto-buy, Discord webhook) stay on your PC and are never touched by updates.
  **Folder** on a macro's card opens where they live.
- The macros only look at the screen and press keys, like a person — they never read or change
  Roblox's memory.

Use at your own risk: macros can be against a game's rules.
